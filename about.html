About Me

Hello! I'm İsa. My software development journey actually started with the question "How does this work?" which I asked as a child, and over time, it transformed into a passionate career with the education I received at Hacı Sevim Yıldız-1 MTAL. Currently, I continue to support this passion academically in the Internet and Network Technologies department at Sinop University.

However, for me, coding is more than just lines on a black screen. Being in the dynamic atmosphere of an advertising agency during my internship allowed me to look at projects not only from a programmer's perspective but also from a designer's point of view.

Today, I create pixel-perfect designs by adding modern tools like React and Tailwind to my HTML, CSS, and JavaScript fundamentals. My Photoshop skills allow me to blend visual details with technical structures. I fuel this frontend strength with my curiosity about backend and data architecture.

I believe there's something new to learn in every project, and I aim for continuous improvement. For me, developing my skills is the most enjoyable way to leave a lasting mark in the digital world.
